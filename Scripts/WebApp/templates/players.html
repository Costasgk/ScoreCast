Val is the Euroleague's own valuation: everything a player did well minus everything they did badly, in one number. TS% is true shooting, which charges a trip to the line the possession it actually costs — the only fair way to compare a slasher with a shooter. P40 scales production to forty minutes, which flatters bench players with small samples, so read it beside minutes rather than instead of them. Last 5 is points and minutes across the player's five most recent games. Three-point percentages carry their attempt count because the percentage alone is meaningless below about fifty of them.
